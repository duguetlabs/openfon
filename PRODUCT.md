# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local business owners who want to configure and test a voice assistant, including German- and English-speaking businesses. They need understandable choices without knowing provider-specific model identifiers. The interface must work on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

OpenFon lets an owner give an assistant business information, choose its voice, test conversations and review what callers needed. The requested redesign makes configuration clearer and avoids repeating assistant-specific settings in workspace settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Assistants have their own conversation instructions, language and voice configuration. Workspace settings provide shared business information, provider connections and post-call processing. Custom configurations support bringing a key for each component. Saved provider profiles are reusable snapshots, not an additional live assistant.

Browser calls, transcripts and debug recordings are implemented. Telephony and individual provider modes have separate acceptance requirements in the launch documentation. Do not imply that source availability or synthetic tests prove live-provider acceptance. Show engine-specific voice catalogs and explain cost tradeoffs only where supported by provider facts; ChatGPT product voices do not establish API availability.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the OpenFon name. The owner requested a cleaner, more user-friendly complete interface and dislikes decorative voice-gender icons.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing application routes, configuration forms, provider catalogs and launch documentation are the product truth. Landing conversations are illustrative, not customer evidence. No comparative quality benchmark is established.

## Product Principles

- Give each setting one understandable home and explain its scope.
- Preserve confirmed-save recovery, unsaved edits, account isolation and BYOK behavior.
- Explain choices with plain language; do not invent ratings, identity or pricing.
- Make testing and reviewing a conversation easy to find.
