# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Small-business operators configuring a voice receptionist, including German- and English-speaking businesses. Desktop and mobile use must remain practical between customers.

## Product Purpose
Help owners prepare business facts and receptionist behavior, rehearse privately, control web availability and review callers’ messages. The reception desk brings the business brief, saved receptionist and browser rehearsal together.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Workspace connections supply independent provider keys and defaults. Receptionists retain their own instructions, language, voice and model selections. Knowledge collections hold approved reusable answers. Connections includes saved voice setups and reviewed portable recipes. Account export and confirmed deletion remain available.

Synthetic checks do not establish live-provider, physical-audio or PSTN acceptance. Production deployment, remote migrations and carrier spending require separate authorization. Runtime hosting uses Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects and D1.

## Brand Commitments
The exact approved handset-f logo and selected Brand Identity page define the visual system. Use Lilita One for short headings and Nunito Sans for reading and controls. Keep operational pages calm and directly usable.

## Product Principles
- Explain each choice in the owner’s language and keep advanced provider controls reachable.
- Preserve saved-state recovery, newer drafts and account isolation.
- Distinguish requested routing, dated deployment evidence and observed call results.
- Require explicit saves and deliberate publication; never invent activity or acceptance.
