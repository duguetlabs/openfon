# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Small-business operators who need a voice receptionist. The interface serves owners working between customers on desktop and mobile.
## Product Purpose
Help a business configure its receptionist, supply business information, try it in a browser, and review messages and callback outcomes.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Backend voice/model choices must remain configurable and portable, including Kataleptic. Behavior is governed by the runtime API contract. Production deployment, remote migrations and carrier spending require separate authorization.
## Brand Commitments
OpenFon. Friendly, simpler customer experience and a complete visual and interaction remake. Use the selected Brand Identity page as visual authority; preserve existing product and safety behavior.
## Product Principles
- Begin with the business owner's job.
- Make the receptionist understandable and testable before requiring technical decisions.
- Keep advanced backend configuration reachable through progressive disclosure.
- Show actual runtime state, never imply a call or provider capability was validated merely by displaying it.
## Open Decisions
Exact business sector is unspecified; default copy must remain sector-neutral. The selected identity and reception-desk structure are implemented in the application.
