## Source release / founder post

We’re building OpenFon: an open-source voice assistant for small businesses.

Give it your opening hours, services, and FAQs. Test a conversation privately, review what it said, and publish a browser call link when you’re ready. If it can’t answer, it can take a message for your team. The knowledge stays editable, and improvements need your approval.

The software is MIT licensed and self-hostable on Cloudflare. Provider capabilities and verification are documented separately; hosting, AI and speech usage have their own costs. Kataleptic, the default inference service, is run by the OpenFon founder and is an optional paid service with a separate account. Cloudflare remains the hosting platform.

This release starts with browser calls. It does not connect your existing phone number or confirm calendar bookings yet. Our initial audience hypothesis is developers and agencies supporting service businesses. We’re looking for practical feedback on the setup and conversation-review workflow, not claiming production readiness or existing customer results.

Source and self-hosting guide: https://github.com/duguetlabs/openfon
