## Source release / founder post

We’re building OpenFon: an open-source voice assistant for small businesses.

Give it your opening hours, services, and FAQs. Test a conversation privately, review what it said, and publish a browser call link when you’re ready. If it can’t answer, it can take a message for your team. The knowledge stays editable, and improvements need your approval.

The software is MIT licensed and self-hostable on Cloudflare. You choose your AI provider; hosting and model usage have their own costs.

This release starts with browser calls. It does not connect your existing phone number or confirm calendar bookings yet. We’re looking for practical feedback on the setup and conversation-review workflow.

Source and self-hosting guide: https://github.com/duguetlabs/openfon

## Pilot invitation draft — individual, consent-based

I’m working on OpenFon, an open-source voice assistant that answers from a business’s own FAQs and takes messages. Would you be interested in testing a browser call link with your opening hours and service information?

It won’t replace your phone number or book appointments. The pilot would focus on whether setup makes sense and whether its answers are useful. We’d review the test together before sharing anything with customers.

## 60–90 second demo script

1. Show the public page. Say: “OpenFon gives your business a browser voice assistant, with knowledge you control.”
2. Open a seeded demo workspace using fictional contact details. Create or open “Workshop front desk”.
3. Show one approved FAQ and one draft item. Explain that drafts are not used until approved.
4. Start a private Test Studio call. Ask the actual configured opening-hours question. Keep the real response, including any delay; do not overdub a better answer.
5. Ask a question absent from the knowledge. Show what actually happens. Do not promise a fallback the recording does not demonstrate.
6. End and review the transcript. Turn a caller question into a draft, add an answer and approve it.
7. Show publishing and the browser call link. Close with “Open source, self-hostable, bring your own AI. Browser calls today.”

Record only with non-sensitive demo data. Label edited footage and illustrative conversations. This script is prepared; a live demo recording is still required.
