# OpenFon brand identity context

The product is a configurable voice receptionist for small businesses. The current customer journey is teach → choose a voice → test in the browser → share → review. The owner supplies approved business information; the assistant can answer questions and take messages. Browser calling, transcripts, callback requests and provider configuration are established product capabilities. Telephone carrier rollout, physical audio acceptance and calendar booking are separate from this brand work.

Primary audience: owners and operators of small service businesses, often working with customers when an inquiry arrives. Secondary audience: agencies and technical implementers who configure assistants for businesses. English and German are relevant languages. Sector, launch geography, pricing, customer counts and commercial performance are not established in the supplied evidence.

The user explicitly approved the Lilita One wordmark with the handset-shaped f, variant 4, and final tightened f–o spacing. This asset is immutable. The user requested a complete brand direction, style and language based on that logo and known product context. The surrounding identity is the first implemented proposal, not an independently user-approved final strategy.
