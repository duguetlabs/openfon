## Overview

**Creative North Star: “A warm welcome. A clear next step.”**

Expand the approved identity: the handset-f, Lilita One and blue/navy pairing stay fixed. The visual world is a friendly reception desk: a confident blue welcome, a butter-yellow message slip and an orderly white working surface. It belongs to an owner in ordinary daylight, between customers. Personality is strongest in the name and short campaign headings; reading and operating surfaces stay clear.

This contract governs the identity kit. It complements the current app’s rehearsal-desk system. The app keeps its existing interaction design and UI font during this task; applying the proposed broader typography across the app is a separate coordinated implementation.

## Colors

Blue carries primary actions and the principal brand field. Ink carries text on white, paper and butter. Butter is the welcoming accent and message-slip surface; it is never a warning merely because it is yellow. Semantic success/error colors remain distinct and always have textual labels. Marketing can use a large blue field; product screens use mostly paper/white with focused blue actions.

Do not set white text on butter or muted text on blue. Contrast measurements for permitted pairs are generated into `assets/contrast.json` and summarized in the guidelines.

## Typography

Lilita One supplies the logo and occasional short display headlines, with no synthetic bold or tight tracking. Nunito Sans supplies the kit’s reading text, subheadings and labels. Its open counters and rounded construction echo the wordmark while remaining useful in paragraphs. It is locally bundled under the SIL Open Font License. Avenir Next/Segoe UI/system sans are fallbacks.

Display 40–72px; section titles 28–40px; subheadings 20–24px; body 16–18px; metadata 14px. Body line height 1.6. Keep body copy around 65 characters per line. Product forms should not use Lilita One.

## Layout

Align most content to a clear left edge. Use an 8px working rhythm, generous section boundaries and compact related content. Marketing may pair a large headline with a message slip; a visual brand book uses full-width specimens and comparison rows, not a dashboard of ornamental cards. At narrow widths, stack specimens and preserve normal text sizes.

## Elevation & Depth

Use flat color fields and whitespace. A single offset shadow may distinguish an illustrative loose message slip. Controls and operational surfaces use a border or a change of background, without floating decorative chrome.

## Shapes

The handset’s bowed stem and compact foot inform a gently curved graphic language. Use an open curved connector to link question → answer → message in explanatory diagrams; never reshape the logo into the connector. Round controls to 8px and message sheets to 16px. Avoid speech-bubble logos, full rotary phones and handwritten wordmarks.

## Components

Primary buttons are blue/white, at least 44px tall, with clear hover and focus states. On blue fields, a butter/ink action is allowed. Inputs have visible labels and clear boundaries. A message slip has a plain heading, the message and a next-step label; illustrative slips must say “Illustrative example.” A status pairs text with color. The identity book’s language controls switch real English/German samples, never simulated live calls.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Use the supplied paths for every logo reproduction; maintain its corrected f–o spacing.
- Keep product copy concrete and distinguish requests from completed outcomes.
- Show owners doing real work; label generated scenes and illustrative dialogue.
- Keep short display headlines expressive and long reading text quiet.
- Do not recolor the logo arbitrarily, distort it, animate its parts or replace its f with a stock handset.
- Do not use sparkles, AI brains, neon gradients or a voice waveform as the brand identifier.
