## Overview

**Creative North Star: The clear working desk.** A local business owner should immediately see where to change an assistant, test it and review a conversation. The replacement identity uses an orderly directory and readable forms, without the old sculptural telephone, textured canvas or display-serif headings. Daytime desktop work and handheld use favor a light canvas with dark readable text.

## Colors

Restrained blue marks actions and selection. White surfaces sit on a pale slate canvas. Red means errors or destructive actions, green means a confirmed success. Color never carries a state alone.

## Typography

System sans throughout, including headings. Page titles are compact and strong; section titles are smaller and sentence case. Labels remain normal-size human language. Monospace is reserved for actual technical values, never section decoration. Supporting text uses 12–13px, form and body text 14–17px, section headings 18–22px, page headings 27–34px and public display headings 36–61px. Fluid sizes keep the same hierarchy on narrow screens.

## Layout

Desktop navigation is a persistent left directory, with the working page to its right. On narrow screens it becomes horizontally scrollable navigation above the content. Main content has a readable maximum width. Forms group related controls; helper text explains scope before choices. Settings use anchor navigation so changing a section does not discard edits.

## Elevation & Depth

Panels use a light border without large shadows. Only floating menus need elevation. Decorative gradients, background noise and perspective objects are absent. Forms render immediately without entrance staggers. Actual call activity may animate, with a reduced-motion fallback.

## Shapes

Controls have modest rounded corners. Sections use generous spacing rather than repeated nested cards. Badges are small and textual.

## Components

Buttons share a consistent minimum target size and visible disabled state. Inputs have stable labels and clear focus rings. Navigation has a textual current-page indicator. Error and success notices preserve recovery controls. Voice identity is a name and verified descriptive text, with a separate play button.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Preserve existing async recovery, unsaved-edit protection and accessible labels.
- Use real costs and qualified tradeoffs instead of decorative star ratings.
- Keep assistant configuration separate from workspace defaults and credentials.
- Do not infer a person's gender or engine compatibility from a familiar voice name.
