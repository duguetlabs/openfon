# OpenFon design

The selected [Brand Identity book](docs/brand/identity/index.html) is the visual authority: exact handset-f wordmark, Lilita One short headlines, Nunito Sans reading text, blue, butter and paper.

The [application design contract](web/src/cleanroom/DESIGN.md) defines the current welcome and reception-desk interface. The [product contract](web/src/cleanroom/PRODUCT.md) and [functional contract](docs/cleanroom-functional-contract.md) define behavior. The earlier remake documents are historical proposals, not current visual authority.

Use the approved SVG geometry without alteration. Operating pages retain native, labeled controls, explicit saves, draft retention and accessible recovery. Deployment and live provider/audio acceptance remain separate from visual and synthetic validation.
